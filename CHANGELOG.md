# Zenkai Changelog

## v1.6.9

### ✨ What's New
- **Movies & TV Series Integration**: Full catalog browsing powered by TMDB Cloudflare Gateway with Direct HD MovieBox playback.
- **Harmonized OTT Experience**: Rebuilt Anime home screen to seamlessly match the Movies, Manga, and Novel layouts.
- **Direct Messaging & Inbox**: Added private 1-on-1 messaging with instant profile caching and a dedicated Messages Inbox accessible directly from your profile.
- **Ascension & Gamification**: Complete quest system with unified level calculation across watched anime hours, read manga chapters, and novel pages.
- **Monochrome Community Chat**: Clean, modern monochrome bubble redesign with optimistic instant sending and auto-scroll to latest.
- **Multi-Source Manga & Novel Readers**: Multi-provider support for WeebCentral, Demonic Scans, Comick, MangaDex, Lnori, NovelFull, and NovelFire.
- **Dub & Sub Audio Engine**: Automatic audio track detection in CinePlayer with instant switching between Sub and Dub tracks.
- **Resilient Background Downloads**: Multi-threaded download service with persistent notification status and storage management.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- Fixed profile UUID flicker on direct message opens by caching user profile data.
- Fixed community scrolling jitter and opening scroll position to focus the latest messages.
- Fixed rectangle background cuts on rounded borders across message bubbles and composer inputs.
- Fixed audio track selection and sync on multi-audio HLS streams.
- Fixed watchlist deletion and sync issues across sessions.
